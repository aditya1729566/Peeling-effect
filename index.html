Behind the surface

Three.js / GLSL / GSAP

A flat image,
taught to lift
like a sticker.

The portrait is drawn as a die-cut texture on a transparent WebGL plane. A fragment shader lifts one corner and hinges the peeled material over the stuck surface as one continuous adhesive-backed sheet.

Scroll is the only control. Move back up and the sheet settles perfectly flat again.
